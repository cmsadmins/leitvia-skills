# Shop pages

Details for `leitvia-shop`.

- **Many product images.** A product with more than three images reads best with all images one below the other next to a box with title, price and buy button that stays in view. An add-on can bring such a layout as a template pattern for `single-product` (a scroll gallery). Apply it with [`leitvia/update-template`](../../../handbook/abilities/leitvia-update-template.md): `template: "single-product"`, a `pattern` and your `package` key. Without a known name, send `pattern: "list"` once: the rejection `leitvia_unknown_pattern` lists in `patterns` the patterns made for the template; with none listed, the layout is an open question. The template applies to every product, so name it in the plan (checkpoint **Plan confirmed**); a rollback brings back the template of the theme.
- **Newsletter sign-up.** No scripts, embeds or forms of a newsletter service on a page: they load the service in the browser of every visitor before any consent. A sign-up is a newsletter block from `leitvia/list-blocks` that passes the address on from the server, and a person sets up its service and key. Without such a block the sign-up is an open question in the report.
