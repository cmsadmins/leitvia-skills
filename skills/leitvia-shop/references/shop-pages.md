# Shop pages

Details for `leitvia-shop`.

- **Many product images.** A product with more than three images reads best with all images one below the other next to a box with title, price and buy button that stays in view. An add-on can bring such a layout as a template pattern for `single-product` (a scroll gallery). Apply it with [`leitvia/update-template`](../../../handbook/abilities/leitvia-update-template.md): `template: "single-product"`, a `pattern` and your `package` key. Without a known name, send `pattern: "list"` once: the rejection `leitvia_unknown_pattern` lists in `patterns` the patterns made for the template; with none listed, the layout is an open question. The template applies to every product, so name it in the plan (checkpoint **Plan confirmed**); a rollback brings back the template of the theme.
- **Variants.** On a variable product the tiles of an add-on steer the select fields of the one buy form of the template; never add a second buy form to the page or the description. A bar with the buy button that stays at the bottom wraps by itself on narrow screens: never shorten product names for it, check it with the [page check](../../leitvia-a11y/references/page-check.md) at 320 px.
- **Payment and other provider plugins.** Many load the script of their provider (a payment SDK) on product and cart pages as soon as they are active, before an account is connected and before any consent. The `foreignHosts` of the page check list them; each one is a step for the person before going live.
- **Shop page and categories.** The start page of the shop is the template `archive-product`; product categories, tags and attributes use it too until they have a template of their own. To lay out categories apart from the start page, write `taxonomy-product_cat` (tags: `taxonomy-product_tag`) with [`leitvia/update-template`](../../../handbook/abilities/leitvia-update-template.md), content with `core/query-title` and the product collection, and leave `archive-product` for the start page. Both apply to every category or every visit of the shop, so name them in the plan; a rollback removes a category template again and categories fall back to `archive-product`.
- **Newsletter sign-up.** No scripts, embeds or forms of a newsletter service on a page: they load the service in the browser of every visitor before any consent. A sign-up is a newsletter block from `leitvia/list-blocks` that passes the address on from the server, and a person sets up its service and key. Without such a block the sign-up is an open question in the report.
