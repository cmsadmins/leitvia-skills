# Shop pages

Details for `leitvia-shop`.

- **Many product images.** A product with more than three images reads best with all images one below the other next to a box with title, price and buy button that stays in view. When `leitvia/list-patterns` lists such a layout of an add-on for the template `single-product` (a scroll gallery), name it in the report as the product page for these products. A person sets the template in the site editor; no ability writes templates.
- **Newsletter sign-up.** No scripts, embeds or forms of a newsletter service on a page: they load the service in the browser of every visitor before any consent. A sign-up is a newsletter block from `leitvia/list-blocks` that passes the address on from the server, and a person sets up its service and key. Without such a block the sign-up is an open question in the report.
