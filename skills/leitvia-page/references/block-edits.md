# Block edits

Details for `leitvia-page`, `leitvia-maintain` and `leitvia-a11y`. Read this when a change goes beyond the text of a block.

- **A new link on words of a text:** `add_link` with `text` (the exact words of one run of text) and `url`, per block in `leitvia/update-block` or `leitvia/update-content`. Other links and inline formatting stay; the first place outside a link counts. `url` alone only changes the link a text has. A new text with a link in it: `text`, `link_text` (the linked words, part of `text`) and `url` in one edit, also in `insert`: `{ "block": "core/paragraph", "text": "Write to hello@example.org.", "link_text": "hello@example.org", "url": "mailto:hello@example.org" }`.
- **Publish a draft:** `leitvia/update-content` with `status: "publish"` and the `package` key, like any change. At a level below `autonomous` it waits for a person. `future` needs a `date` in the future; without a date of its own the post gets the date of publishing.
- **Text attributes of static blocks:** `leitvia/update-block` with `attributes` changes prefix, value, suffix and label of `leitvia/counter`, and any text attribute whose element the block has. An optional part that is empty, other than on the counter, is refused with `leitvia_static_markup`.
- **Ground of a light section:** `attributes: { "backgroundColor": "surface-alt" }` on the outer `core/group` of a section whose ground is light: `surface` or `surface-alt`, `null` removes it. Use it when two neighbors would share a tone. A dark band is `attributes: { "className": "is-style-inverse" }`; other grounds come with another pattern.
- **Size of a heading or paragraph:** `attributes: { "fontSize": "<slug>" }` with a font size of `leitvia/get-tokens`, `null` for the size of the theme. The rejection lists the slugs. The same on a `core/quote` makes the quotes of a row equal.
- **Color of a heading or paragraph:** `attributes: { "textColor": "accent" }`, also `ink` or `ink-muted`, `null` for the color of the section; such as a card title in accent.
- **Icon color and size:** `attributes: { "textColor": "accent", "fontSize": "heading-m" }` on `leitvia/icon` or `leitvia/icon-list`; the icon is 1em of the size (`heading-m` about 32 px, `heading-l` about 40 px).
- **Height of a cover:** `attributes: { "minHeight": 90 }` on a `core/cover`, in vh from 30 to 100, `null` for the height of its content.
- **A block the pattern lacks, or another order inside a section:** an edit with `insert` adds a block after the one named, with `position: "before"` ahead of it; `insert` takes `core/paragraph`, `core/heading`, `core/separator` and `core/site-logo` besides the catalog, with `text` and `attributes` (`className`, `level`). An eyebrow above a heading: `{ "block": "<heading>", "insert": { "block": "core/paragraph", "text": "Services", "attributes": { "className": "is-style-eyebrow" } }, "position": "before" }`. `move` with the ID of a sibling moves a block next to it (`position` after or before). Each is an edit of its own; IDs after it shift. A section takes up to 250 edits. A list with any number of entries: `insert` of `leitvia/icon-list` with `inner` (one `leitvia/icon-list-item` with `text` each), or `repeat` on one entry or card and edits of the copies.
- **Tables:** `table` with `head` (column headers), `rows` (each as long as `head`) and for `core/table` `foot` fills every cell as plain text, on `core/table` (such as in `content-price-list`, `content-comparison`) and on `leitvia/comparison-table`, also in `insert`. Column headers get `scope="col"`, the first cell of a row becomes its row header (`row_headers: false` for none). Put the caption in `caption` of the same edit; a `leitvia/comparison-table` inserted without one has no caption (none of the English sample text of its example), so name it after the section. Core tables scroll inside a focusable region at 390 px, the comparison table turns into cards. In the block style `is-style-comparison` only columns of figures and prices align to the end, columns of words stay at the start; no alignment per cell needed.
- **Two block styles on one block:** `className` takes up to three styles that combine, space separated, such as `"is-style-sticky-header-desktop is-style-overlay-header"` on the header group. `overlay-header` sticks like every header; `sticky-header` is the default and needs no class.
- **Columns of a gallery:** `columns` 1 to 6 on the `core/gallery` block, as on a grid.
- **Name for screen readers:** `attributes: { "label": "..." }` on a slider, an announcement bar or the accessibility bar changes the `aria-label` in their markup. Write it in the language of the page.
- **Posts per page of a list:** `leitvia/update-block` on the `core/query` block with `attributes: { "query": { "perPage": 6 } }`. Keys you leave out stay. `perPage` counts with `inherit` false.
- **A new section on a page that exists:** `leitvia/insert-section` with `post`, `sections` (each `pattern` and `edits` as in `leitvia/compose-page`, with the same checks) and one place: `after` or `before` with a top-level ID from the outline, or `position` `start` or `end`; without a place the sections go to the end. An anchor must be unique on the whole page.
- **A hero for a page that exists, with the template page:** `leitvia/insert-section` with the hero in `sections`, `position: "start"` and `template: "page-landing"` in one call. The hero carries the h1 and the new template prints no title, so the page keeps exactly one h1; the other blocks stay as they are. Never `compose-page` with `post` for this when the page holds blocks of other plugins.
- **A block of another plugin in a section:** `leitvia/insert-section` with `wrap: { "block": "<top-level ID>", "ground": "surface-alt", "heading": "Book a call" }` puts a top-level block (a shortcode, a form or calendar of a plugin) unchanged into a section at its place: full width, the spacing of a section, ground `surface` (default), `surface-alt` or `inverse`, and an optional h2 at the wide edge, where the headings of the patterns start. No place with `wrap`.
- **Another order of sections:** `leitvia/insert-section` with `move` (top-level ID of the section) and the place, one call per section; read the outline again in between, path IDs shift.
- **Take a section out:** `leitvia/remove-section` with `block`. The last section of a post stays; `leitvia/rollback` of the package brings a section back.
- **Another pattern for a section:** `leitvia/replace-pattern` with `block` (a top-level ID from the outline), `pattern` and `edits` as in `leitvia/compose-page`. Texts and button links of the old section move over first, the edits win; `carry: false` keeps the texts of the pattern. Set an anchor again with an `anchor` edit.
- **Page under another page:** `parent` of `leitvia/update-content`, `0` for the top level. It changes the address like a slug; point old links to it with `leitvia/repair-links` and `from` and `to`.
- **Remove a page or post:** `leitvia/trash-content`. It always waits for a person and stays restorable; no ability deletes for good, the person empties the trash. Refused for the front page, the posts page, the privacy page and a page with subpages; `notice` names the pages that link to it.
- **Map (`leitvia/map`):** set `lat`, `lng`, `zoom` (1 to 19), `height`, `heading`, `linkText` and `markers` (each `title`, `address`, `lat`, `lng`) as `attributes` of `insert` or of `leitvia/update-block`; the list of places is built again. `markers` replaces the list, so send it on every insert, or the sample place stays. `text` is the heading.
- **Bound button:** a button whose `url` a binding fills (`metadata.bindings.url`, for example the button of a job posting) takes no new link. `url: ""` removes only its saved link, the binding stays; change the value at its source.
- **Library image:** `image` `{ id }` without `alt` keeps the alt text of the library; `decorative: true` for decoration.
- **Caption:** `caption` on `leitvia/update-block` or per block in `leitvia/update-content`, plain text, on images, videos, audio, galleries, tables and embeds. An empty string removes it.

## Captions of a video

A video with speech needs captions (WCAG 1.2.2):

1. `leitvia/add-media` with `data` (Base64) and `filename` ending in `.vtt`: UTF-8, first line `WEBVTT`. No alt text. It needs the right to upload files, and the result gives the attachment ID in `target.post`; in a package, in `applied.post` of `leitvia/get-package` once it is applied.
2. `leitvia/update-block` on the `core/video` block with `attributes: { "tracks": [ { "id": <attachment>, "kind": "captions", "srclang": "de", "label": "Deutsch" } ] }`. The list replaces the tracks of the video, an empty list removes them. Only WebVTT files of the library count.

The file must lie on this site, or browsers do not load it.
