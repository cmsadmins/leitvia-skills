# Text gates

Every text you write for a page, a post, a title, an excerpt, a menu or an alt text passes two gates before you send it: one against machine-written style, one for search engines and AI answers. Check your draft against both lists and rewrite until nothing is left; a text that fails is not ready, even when the ability would accept it. The gates apply to the words you write. Wording you move unchanged (a migration) keeps its words; list what it would fail in your report.

## Anti-AI gate

- No dashes as punctuation: no em dash, no en dash (also not in ranges, write "10 to 20"), no spaced hyphen between words. Use a comma, brackets, a colon or a new sentence. A hyphen inside a word ("block-based") is fine.
- No arrows (the arrow characters and `->`), no emojis. Icons come from the theme or a pattern, never from text.
- No stock phrases. English: seamless, delve, leverage, robust, game-changer, unlock, elevate, "in today's", "in the world of", "dive into", deep dive, harness, empower, cutting-edge, state-of-the-art, furthermore, moreover, "in conclusion", comprehensive, effortless, streamline, supercharge, revolutionize, "next level", "it's important to note", "look no further", tapestry, embark, realm, unleash. German: nahtlos, umfassend, zusammenfassend, abschliessend as a sentence opener, "darueber hinaus", "in der heutigen Zeit", "tauchen wir ein", eintauchen, "kurz gesagt", letztendlich, "in diesem Zusammenhang", "zu guter Letzt", reibungslos, ganzheitlich, massgeschneidert, bahnbrechend, revolutionaer, entfesseln (each with its real umlauts and sharp s in the text). Say the concrete thing instead: what it does, for whom, with a figure.
- German texts use real umlauts and the sharp s, never ae, oe, ue or ss in their place (fuer, ueber, moeglich, Groesse are wrong). Only names and code keep their spelling.
- Name an AI agent as such in news and marketing texts: "AI agent" or "KI-Agent", or AI or KI in the same paragraph. A bare "agent" is ambiguous (a travel agent, an agency, a user agent). Product names are no bare agent: Agent-Gateway, Agent gateway, Agent-Skills, Agent skills, Leitvia Agent.
- No sentences that say nothing: no summary of what the section just said, no outlook, no closing line. Vary the sentence length; not three sentences in a row that start the same way.

## SEO and GEO gate

| What | Rule |
|---|---|
| Title | At most 60 characters, the topic first. It is the h1. |
| Description (excerpt) | 120 to 160 characters, one or two full sentences from the text, with a figure where the text has one. Legal pages need one, any length. |
| h1 | Exactly one per page; posts get theirs from the title, so the content has none. |
| h2 | A text of more than 300 words has h2 sections, each heading names its section. No heading level is skipped (h2 to h4). |
| Images | Every image has an alt text that says what it shows, or is marked decorative. |
| Internal links | A post links at least one page or post of the same site on its topic (leitvia-news asks for two). |
| First paragraph | In a news post at most 60 words, with at least one figure or date: who, what, when, where, why. It makes sense on its own, so AI answers can quote it. |
| Languages | On a site with several languages every page and post has its twin in each language, written in that language, not left out. |

A text that cannot meet a rule without facts you do not have (a figure for the first paragraph, a page to link) is an open question in your report, never an invented fact.
